Kinetics first: springs and clutches do the work, one optional motor climbs

130 kg / 1.87 m wearer, side view facing right. Left: walking (mid-stance, near leg). Right: chair mode, 10° recline. Numbers per leg / per side, from KINETICS.md (EST until measured).

Build first (no motors, ~4.4 kg, ~$1,300): Dock 0.7 kg; M1 Knee/Seat 2.9 kg (sit anywhere, 28 Nm stand-up spring); M2 Back/Lift 0.35 kg (60 Nm, ~12 kg lighter box); M3 Hip bands 0.35 kg (published −7% walking); running band 50 g (−6.4% running).
Then upgrades: smart clutches (+0.3 kg, ~$600: electroadhesive, fail open); M4 ankle (+0.9 kg, −7% walking); M5 shoulder (+1.6 kg); couplings appear when modules dock: hamstring, calf, squat differential, shoulder-back.
One motor (P1, ~6.7 kg, ~$3,000): only for stairs up, uphill and heavy lifts. Stoop lift goes from 60 to 110 Nm rated / 154 Nm peak (box ~22 / 31 kg lighter). A second motor adds almost nothing: the thigh pad caps it near 160 Nm.
