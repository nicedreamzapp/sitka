Tendon concept: sit on a stick under each sit bone, the knee tendon only trims

Chair mode, leaned back 10°, 130 kg / 1.87 m wearer, side view facing right, one leg shown (both identical). Bones carry the squeeze; the suit adds only pull, plus a few small stiff parts. Numbers are per leg, from TENDON_CONCEPT.md.
Sitting: the strut stands almost straight under each sit bone and takes 69% of your weight through a seat cup (24 kPa, bike-saddle range). The knee tendon holds the last 34 Nm: 426 N of pull, about 43 kg.
Stiff parts that stay: two struts, two seat cups, the knee hinges with the 80 mm arc, the clutch, a thin back panel and the hub. Everything else is cable, webbing and thin contoured shells.
If the clutch slips: a separate backup strap with stretch catches you about 2° deeper (1.6 cm), and the strut is still under you. Lean forward or deep-squat and the knee load climbs to 48–72 Nm: shorter sits only.
